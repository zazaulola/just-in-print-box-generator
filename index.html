In place: one pre-assembled closed piece; the pins are grown inside the hinges with 0.45 mm clearance — the first turn breaks them free; print in any orientation (e.g. tilted ~45°). Filament pin: box and lid print separately and flat; push 1.75 mm filament through the ⌀2.1 channel and melt the ends. Snap-on: the lid clips onto rods once (hold it upright at ~90° and press straight down) and only comes off deliberately. The flap latch swings on a grown mini-hinge over a wall lug; the tongue latch clicks over a rib on the front wall.
The lid slides into side slots from one end; friction bumps hold it shut and a grip bar helps pull it open. Both parts print flat, no supports.
