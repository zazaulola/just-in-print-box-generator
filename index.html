Prints as a single pre-assembled piece, closed: a flat lid hinged along the top back edge, plus a swinging latch flap on its own mini-hinge at the front, hanging on a lug in the wall (to open the box, flip the flap up). The pins are grown inside the hinges with 0.45 mm clearance — the first turn breaks them free. Print orientation is up to you: tilt ~45° around the long axis, supports as you like — they won't reach into the hinge gaps.
